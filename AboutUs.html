About Us

We are Pixel Squad, a group of students who share a love for video games. We created GameZone to help visitors learn about gaming, from popular game genres to the exciting world of esports.

Our Purpose

Gaming is more than a pastime. It brings people together, builds teamwork and quick thinking, and can even become a career. Through GameZone, we want beginners to discover new games and fans to learn something new about the games they already love.
